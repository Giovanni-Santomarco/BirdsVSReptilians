# [BirdsVSReptilians]

A top-down roguelite shooter made in Unity.

### 👥 Authors
Developed by:
* **Carlo Maria Proietti**
* **Giovanni Santomarco**

---

### 🚀 Killer Features
* **Procedural Generation:** Explore randomly generated levels that scale in difficulty.
* **Smart Combat:** Face off against complex enemy AI designed to hunt you down.
* **Many Guns:** Discover all the 22 different guns.

---

### 🐢 The Story
The rules are simple: you are a turtle whose only desire is to hunt down as many birds as possible. Clear each level to advance, but watch your back—those birds will do whatever it takes to strike you down and maintain their spot at the top of the food chain.

If luck is on your side, fallen birds will drop high-tech, powerful weapons and medkits to keep you breathing.

**Push your limits and see how high of a level you can reach!**
